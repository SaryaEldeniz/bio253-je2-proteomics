## Important interpretation finding

Source estimate_type flags are model-wide in this export. In the ancestor
comparison, 25 ordinary entries labelled lod_imputed have 3/3 values in BOTH
relevant groups. Conversely an observed model can have sparse relevant groups.
The code now displays actual group counts alongside the unchanged source flag.
Earlier shorthand that every LOD-flagged contrast implies a missing contrasted
group is too strong and should not be used.

Native TSB_vs_SN <0 means higher in SN. With R=SN-TSB, the interactions are
R_anc-R_stk, R_anc-R_stp and R_stk-R_stp. The third equals the second minus the
first for effects only; its FDR/statistic/error are not obtained by subtraction.
