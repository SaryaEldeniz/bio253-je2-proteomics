## Question and agreed scope

Which abundance-response modules are associated with long PASN exposure and
Stk1/Stp1 mutant backgrounds? Use the October total-proteome export and the
resources introduced in 06_web_resources_2026.pdf. The user confirmed October
and KEGG/GO/STRING. Earlier source sample validation remains skipped.

## What ran

The Step 4 source effects, statistics and FDRs were preserved. Prepared a primary
universe of 2,058 high-quality singleton UniProt ortholog mappings, with local
JE2/STRING-v12 term memberships transferred through that map. The exact snapshot
minor release and GO/KEGG dates are unknown; input hashes retain its identity.

Tested KEGG, GO BP/MF/CC and UniProt keywords in ten planned comparisons. The
primary universe supplies 818 eligible categories per comparison, with effective
set sizes 10-500. ORA separates positive/negative source hits (FDR<0.1 and
absolute log2 effect>1); GSEA ranks all eligible signed source DEA statistics.

Used upper-tail hypergeometric ORA and fgseaMultilevel 1.32.4, fixed seeds,
serial backend, sampleSize101, nPermSimple1000 and eps1e-10. Recorded p-value
floors, uncertainty and warnings. BH correction is separate by method/policy
across all ten comparisons and collections, with local corrections retained.

Repeated with >=2/3 measurements per relevant group, that restriction plus the
source observed label, and full eligible local-JE2 IDs. Each policy has a matched
foreground/rank vector/background. Completed 64,212 ORA and 32,106 GSEA tests
across all policies. Primary results have 186 significant ORA term-comparisons
and 1,641 significant GSEA term-comparisons at study FDR<0.1. These overlap and
are not counts of independent biological pathways.

## Main findings to investigate

Purine metabolism/de novo IMP biosynthesis is the clearest kinase-dependent
module. Purine GSEA has stk-versus-ancestor interaction NES+1.932/FDR0.000696
and stp-versus-stk NES-1.786/FDR0.00512, persisting across all policies. Sample
plots show lower purine-enzyme abundance in long SN, with a stronger fall in
stk. The stp-versus-ancestor whole-purine interaction is not significant.

Nitrogen metabolism is a second robust stp-versus-ancestor GSEA result
(NES+1.838/FDR0.0257). Its nitrate-related contributors have measured values in
all relevant replicates. Inspect absolute values and starting differences:
an interaction describes response differences, not necessarily lower final SN
abundance. Further interpretation, oxidative-phosphorylation results and the
ORA-only BCAA caveat are in reports/step05/interpretation.md.

## Limits and next phase

Abundance enrichment does not prove activity, flux or direct phosphorylation.
Stp1 measurements vary, and Stk1 has no detections in stp SN, so stp is not
automatically a clean isolated phosphatase effect. Phosphosite localization,
parent-protein checks, directional Venn overlaps and sequence logos remain for
the later phosphosite workflow. Prioritize supported modules when choosing sites.
