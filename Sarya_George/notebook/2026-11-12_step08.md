# 2026-11-12 — Step 8 · STYH overlap in TSB and SN

## Method

Codex compared `stk_vs_anc_at_TSB` with `stp_vs_anc_at_TSB` and repeated the comparison for `stk_vs_anc_at_SN` and `stp_vs_anc_at_SN`. The input was `data/derived/dea_phospho_STYH.tsv.gz`. We used **FDR < 0.10** and **|log₂FC| > 1** and excluded entries marked as contaminants in the supplied DEA. LOD-imputed estimates were included when they met the same thresholds.

Overlap was counted by unique phosphosite ID. Among shared sites, matching signs indicate effects in the same direction relative to `anc` within that medium. The circles are schematic and their labels give the counts.

## Results

| Medium | Only `stk` | Both | Only `stp` | Shared, same direction | Shared, opposite direction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| TSB | 40 | 20 | 56 | 18 | 2 |
| SN | 13 | 32 | 31 | 31 | 1 |

The shared set contained 20 sites in TSB and 32 in SN. Most shared effects had the same sign relative to `anc` in each medium.
