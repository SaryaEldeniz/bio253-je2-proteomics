# 2026-11-13 — Step 9 · STYH residue counts and sequence-logo illustration

## Analysis

We counted pS, pT, pY and pH in the four Step 8 contrasts in `data/derived/dea_phospho_STYH.tsv.gz`. Testable entries had finite effect and FDR estimates. We excluded source-marked contaminants. The hit thresholds were **FDR < 0.10** and **|log₂FC| > 1**. The table and bar chart retain the original `observed` and `lod_imputed` labels.

| Contrast | pS | pT | pY | pH | All significant | LOD-imputed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `stk_vs_anc_at_TSB` | 20 | 22 | 11 | 7 | 60 | 55 |
| `stp_vs_anc_at_TSB` | 22 | 38 | 10 | 6 | 76 | 70 |
| `stk_vs_anc_at_SN` | 12 | 26 | 4 | 3 | 45 | 38 |
| `stp_vs_anc_at_SN` | 18 | 34 | 5 | 6 | 63 | 55 |

Each contrast included 1,749 testable pS, 1,432 pT, 638 pY and 416 pH entries. pT contributed the most hits by count in all four contrasts. Counts depend on the number of testable sites per residue. Every significant pH estimate carried the LOD-imputed flag. The total hits in each contrast match the corresponding set size in the Step 8 Venn diagrams.
