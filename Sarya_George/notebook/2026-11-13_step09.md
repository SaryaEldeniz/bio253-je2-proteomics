# 2026-11-13 — Step 9 · STYH residue counts and position-0 logo

## Analysis

We counted pS, pT, pY and pH in the four Step 8 contrasts in `data/derived/dea_phospho_STYH.tsv.gz`. Testable entries had finite effect and FDR estimates. We excluded source-marked contaminants. The hit thresholds were **FDR < 0.10** and **|log₂FC| > 1**. The table and bar chart retain the original `observed` and `lod_imputed` labels.

| Contrast | pS | pT | pY | pH | All significant | LOD-imputed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `stk_vs_anc_at_TSB` | 20 | 22 | 11 | 7 | 60 | 55 |
| `stp_vs_anc_at_TSB` | 22 | 38 | 10 | 6 | 76 | 70 |
| `stk_vs_anc_at_SN` | 12 | 26 | 4 | 3 | 45 | 38 |
| `stp_vs_anc_at_SN` | 18 | 34 | 5 | 6 | 63 | 55 |

Each contrast included 1,749 testable pS, 1,432 pT, 638 pY and 416 pH entries. pT contributed the most hits by count in all four contrasts. Counts depend on the number of testable sites per residue. Every significant pH estimate carried the LOD-imputed flag. The total hits in each contrast match the corresponding set size in the Step 8 Venn diagrams. [Detailed count table](../reports/step09_phospho_residue_counts.tsv) · [Bar chart](../figures/step09/figure09_phospho_residue_counts.png)

The bar chart stacks the observed count in blue and the LOD-imputed count in orange. Each nonzero count is labeled at the top of its own segment.

## Venn diagrams by residue

Within TSB and SN, we used the same hit thresholds and restricted both mutant-versus-ancestor sets to entries labeled `observed` by the source DEA. For each residue, we counted the sites found in only one of the two sets using the supplied STYH site IDs. The middle of each Venn diagram is unlabelled. Exclusivity means that the site met the selection criteria in one contrast. The site can still be present in both strains. [Venn diagrams](../figures/step09/figure09_phospho_residue_venn.png) · [Venn counts](../reports/step09_phospho_residue_venn_counts.tsv) · [R script](../scripts/05_figures/09_render_residue_venn.R)

The figure shows residue and medium combinations with at least one exclusive observed hit. The count table includes all combinations.

## Position-0 logo

The logo uses the exclusive significant STYH hits labelled Source observed in the Venn count table. It shows the phosphorylated residue at position 0 for each medium and mutant. Letter height gives the within-group residue frequency. The four panels contain 3, 4, 3 and 4 hits, respectively. The small groups support descriptive comparisons of residue frequencies. Sequence context is outside the scope of this figure. [Position-0 logo](../figures/step09/figure09_central_residue_logo.png) · [R script](../scripts/05_figures/09_render_central_residue_logo.R)
