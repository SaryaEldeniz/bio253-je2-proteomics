## Read the rings

| Position | Interaction effect | Positive means |
|---|---|---|
| Inner | stk/anc = R_anc - R_stk | SN response is more positive in anc than stk |
| Middle | stp/anc = R_anc - R_stp | SN response is more positive in anc than stp |
| Outer | stp/stk = R_stk - R_stp | SN response is more positive in stk than stp |

R is the model SN-minus-TSB response. Blue is negative; white is zero; orange-red is positive. Grey means no suitable quantitative estimate, including source-LOD/other models or fewer than2/3 relevant detections; it is not a zero. Black dots show supplied protein FDR<0.1. Numeric estimates, source-model labels and detection counts remain in node CSVs. Display colours alone are clipped outside+/-2; data values are unchanged.

The three contrasts are algebraically dependent and are not independent replication. These are total-protein abundances, not phosphorylation or enzyme activity. STRING confidence represents support for an association, not interaction strength. Central placement and an edge do not establish direct binding or Stk1/Stp1 substrate relationships.

## Reproduce

Open Sarya_George.Rproj. Required local course paths are configured as in earlier steps. The R scripts reuse existing project packages; no graph-package installation is required.

```r

source("scripts/06_network/06_prepare_complete75.R")
```
