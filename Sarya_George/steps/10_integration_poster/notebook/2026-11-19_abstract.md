`Lecture_Abstracts.pdf` and the research guidance in `Lecture_Biblio_Tools.pdf`.

inspected. The student subsequently instructed us to ignore the poster, so no
poster-title coordination or poster edits were pursued.

The draft follows general background, specific background, knowledge gap,
“Here we show”, results and implications. Its 261-word body is within the
300-word limit; body plus the 13-word working title totals 274 words.
References and the recipe/evidence checklist are separate from the body.
No author or affiliation details were inferred.

The main result is the mutant-dependent metabolic protein response, especially
stronger purine-protein decline and respiratory-protein increase in the kinase
mutant. Four observed-only pathway groups pass enrichment FDR <0.1 across five
pathway/contrast pairs. Purine has support from rank-based and overrepresentation
analyses. IsaA/Pyk/QoxB overlap is included as a candidate finding, with explicit
limits: their significant site comparisons are in control broth; none of the
nine corresponding phosphosite interactions is significant. Reduced Stk1 in
the stp strain limits enzyme-specific interpretation. No claims of pathway
activity, improved fitness or newly validated direct substrates were made.

judged by experimental relevance rather than impact factor. Core references are
Schwyter 2025, Prust 2021 and Huemer 2023, with Niggli 2023 and Roman-Rodriguez
2025 as supporting context. Exact PubMed metadata for five records was retrieved
through EFetch, validated against expected DOIs, and exported to TSV and RIS for

failed before permission was granted; the subsequent R run completed. Only
public citation identifiers were sent, and the raw response remains ignored.
